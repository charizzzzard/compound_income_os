## MVP Purpose

This packet reviews the deterministic Monthly Portfolio Decision Brief MVP.
The producer consolidates already-generated CIOS evidence into a compact monthly
operator-facing review surface:

- monthly ranking rows are preserved from upstream output and not recalculated;
- missing mandatory ranking evidence produces `BLOCKED`;
- missing or degraded optional review evidence remains visible and produces
  `REVIEW` where applicable;
- Data Freshness, Decision Quality, Portfolio Health and Review Queue evidence
  are surfaced without changing their producer semantics;
- generated real monthly decision brief outputs remain local/generated by
  default.

This MVP is standalone. It does not alter `personal_run_engine` stage order or
the existing Monthly Decision Report behavior.
