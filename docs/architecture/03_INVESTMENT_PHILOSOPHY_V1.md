# Investment Philosophy v1

Compound Income OS supports a long-horizon portfolio style built around:

1. Dividend Growth
2. Quality Compounders
3. Value Discipline

The system is designed for investment review, not trading.

## What It Is

Dividend Growth means the system cares about sustainable and growing shareholder
cash returns. Dividend safety, payout coverage and free-cash-flow support remain
future research concerns, but the current implementation does not require a heavy
DCF or owner-earnings model before decisions can be reviewed.

Quality Compounders means the system gives attention to durable businesses,
capital allocation, financial quality and long holding periods.

Value Discipline means valuation acts as a guardrail and timing discipline. It is
not a promise of precise fair value.

## What It Is Not

The strategy is not:

- Graham deep value as the primary style
- Greenblatt quant screening as the primary style
- short-term trading
- maximum activity
- a broker/order execution process
- a blackbox optimizer

## Decision Principles

No-action is valid when valuation is unattractive, evidence is incomplete,
portfolio fit is weak, opportunity cost is poor, operator state is unfavorable or
the asset is outside the circle of competence.

Opportunity cost is always relevant. A decision should be compared with a clear
alternative such as cash, a core ETF, an existing holding, a watchlist candidate
or deliberate no-action.

Margin of safety is judgment and discipline, not fake precision. It protects
against wrong assumptions, incomplete data, balance-sheet risk, regime changes
and unknowns.

Scores are diagnostic signals only. A high score can create attention; it cannot
create a final investment decision.

The human operator makes the final decision.

## Practical Constraints

The OS must be usable with limited operator time. A required process that takes
too long or asks for false precision will not be maintained. Research artifacts
should therefore stay small until real usage proves that more structure is worth
the maintenance cost.
