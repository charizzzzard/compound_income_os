## A. Brand Foundation

### Brand essence
**The discipline of compounding, made auditable.**

A single idea that runs through every page, button, and feature description: serious long-term investing is a craft, the craft requires evidence and repetition, and the product is the workshop where that craft happens — locally, on your machine, on your terms.

### Positioning statement
For technically sophisticated long-term investors who refuse to outsource portfolio judgment to opaque SaaS, **Compound Income OS** is a local-first portfolio research operating system that converts broker exports, fundamentals files, and evidence inputs into reproducible rankings, dashboards, and monthly decision reports — without executing trades and without offering advice.

Unlike cloud robo-advisors, screener subscriptions, or trading-signal platforms, Compound Income OS keeps your data local, makes coverage gaps visible, and treats every decision as a documented artifact rather than a one-way action.

### Value proposition
A reproducible monthly process for serious long-term investors:

- **Inputs you control:** broker CSVs, your own fundamentals master, your own evidence files.
- **Deterministic outputs:** scores, rankings, dashboards, snapshot and decision reports — as CSV and Markdown.
- **Visible data quality:** missing, partial, and review cases are surfaced, never silently filled.
- **Local-first by default:** no upload, no account, no broker connection.

### One-liner
**A local operating system for long-term investing.**

Alternates depending on context:
- Conversational: *Your portfolio, your evidence, your decisions — reproducible every month.*
- Technical: *Local-first portfolio research. Deterministic CSV and Markdown artifacts. No broker execution.*
- Audience-led: *Built for dividend-growth and quality-compounder investors who treat their portfolio like infrastructure.*

### Elevator pitch (≈60 seconds)
Long-term investing decays in slow motion. Position sizes drift, theses age out, and the reasoning behind last year’s buys quietly disappears into screenshots and lost browser tabs.

Compound Income OS is a local-first research system that fixes this at the workflow level. You feed it broker exports, your fundamentals master, your watchlist, and your evidence files. It produces a reproducible set of artifacts: portfolio snapshot, scoring, watchlist ranking, monthly decision report, dividend snowball analysis, and a dashboard layer — all as plain CSV and Markdown, all on your machine.

It does not connect to brokers. It does not place orders. It does not invent missing fundamentals. What it does is make your monthly decision process repeatable, your data-quality gaps visible, and your reasoning recoverable next quarter — and the quarter after that.

### Brand personality
A quiet, rigorous instrument. The Bloomberg Terminal’s introverted cousin. An investor who reads 10-Ks for fun and keeps a paper journal next to a Linux laptop.

| Trait | Means | Doesn’t mean |
|---|---|---|
| Calm | Confident understatement; no exclamation marks | Cold, lifeless |
| Precise | Concrete nouns (CSV, manifest, coverage) | Jargon-heavy or academic |
| Rigorous | Surfaces gaps and uncertainty | Hedged, evasive |
| Patient | Built around monthly cycles, multi-year horizons | Slow or sluggish |
| Open | Reproducible artifacts, open-source core | Promiscuous with data |
| Sober | No hype, no return language | Boring or apologetic |

### Tone of voice
- **Declarative.** Short sentences. Active verbs.
- **Specific.** Use the real vocabulary of the product (manifest, coverage, REVIEW, MISSING_DATA, evidence registry) instead of generic SaaS metaphors.
- **Restrained.** Never promise outcomes. Describe behavior.
- **Numerate.** Numbers, intervals, and statuses are part of the voice.
- **Quietly confident.** Tools earn trust by working, not by shouting.

**Avoid:** *empower, unlock, supercharge, seamless, intelligent, smart, effortless, transform, next-generation, game-changing, revolutionary, AI-driven (without a real referent), insights (as filler).*

**Prefer:** *reproducible, deterministic, local, manifest, coverage, evidence, gates, artifacts, journal, discipline, practice, visible, traceable.*

### Messaging pillars

1. **Local-first by default.** Your data stays on your machine. No cloud account is required to use the core.
2. **Evidence over opinion.** Missing fundamentals are never invented. Coverage and review gaps are surfaced as first-class outputs.
3. **Reproducibility as a feature.** Each run produces a manifest of inputs used and artifacts emitted. Re-runs are deterministic.
4. **Monthly discipline, not daily noise.** A cadence designed for long-horizon investing, not for tickers and trading screens.
5. **Transparent, not predictive.** No claims about returns, alpha, or market direction. The system describes what it sees and how it ranks — and shows its work.
6. **Open-source core, paid where it earns its keep.** Core stays open and inspectable. Pro Modules and Setup Service are optional.

## B. Homepage Narrative — Story Arc

A five-beat arc that the page should walk a reader through in well under sixty seconds.

**1. Problem — the slow erosion of investing discipline.**
Long-term portfolios don’t fail dramatically. They fail by drift. Reasoning decays. Spreadsheets sprawl. Evidence gets lost between PDFs, screenshots, and memory.

**2. Reframe — the portfolio as infrastructure.**
The fix is not a better dashboard. It’s a workflow that runs the same way every month, on inputs you control, with outputs you can re-read in two years.

**3. The operating system.**
Compound Income OS turns your local files into a reproducible pipeline: snapshot, score, rank, evidence-check, dividend-impact, decide, document.

**4. Evidence and gates.**
Coverage is visible. Missing data stays missing — flagged, not hidden. SEC refreshes are explicit and reviewed. Nothing in the master is mutated silently.

**5. Monthly decision discipline → conversion.**
Each cycle ends with a Markdown decision report and a journal entry. Join Early Access. Request GitHub Access. Or have the team set it up for you.

### 2. Problem — *“Where long-term portfolios actually fail.”*

> Most long-term portfolios don’t fail in market crashes. They fail in slow motion.
>
> Position sizes drift past the limits you set. Theses age out without anyone noticing. Evidence — the screenshots, the 10-K passages, the reason you bought — scatters across folders, browsers, and memory. By the time conviction matters, the reasoning behind it is already gone.
>
> Spreadsheets help, until they sprawl. Dashboards help, until they’re cloud-locked. Screeners help, until you realize a screener can’t document a decision.
>
> What’s missing isn’t more data. It’s a process that runs the same way every month and leaves a paper trail.

### 3. Solution — *“The portfolio as local infrastructure.”*

> Compound Income OS is that process.
>
> It is a local-first research operating system that takes the files you already have — broker positions, fundamentals data, your watchlist, your evidence — and runs them through a deterministic pipeline. The outputs are CSV and Markdown artifacts: a portfolio snapshot, scoring, watchlist ranking, a monthly decision report, dividend impact analysis, and a dashboard layer.
>
> Inputs you control. Outputs you can re-read in two years. Nothing uploaded, nothing executed, nothing invented.

### 4. Product Pillars — *“Six principles, enforced by the architecture.”*

A six-card section. Short labels, single-line elaborations.

| Pillar | One-line copy |

### 6. Monthly Workflow — *“A cadence built for long horizons.”*

A six-step horizontal flow. Each step is a card with a short label and one or two lines of copy.

1. **Broker export in.** Drop your broker CSV into the inputs folder. The position parser normalizes it.
2. **Data quality check.** Coverage is computed against your fundamentals master. Missing required KPIs are flagged before scoring.
3. **Scoring & ranking.** Business, valuation, and buy scores are applied. Watchlist and monthly rankings produced.
4. **Dividend impact.** The dividend snowball analysis updates projected income against your current weights and rules.
5. **Monthly decision report.** A Markdown report explains why each candidate is eligible, deferred, or blocked — with the data behind it.
6. **Decision journal.** Your reasoning is captured as an artifact, not a tab you’ll close.

---

### 7. Dividend Snowball Analysis

> The dividend snowball view tracks projected income across your current holdings and watchlist, against your portfolio rules and cash position.
>
> It is not a forecast and not a yield-trap detector. It is a transparent calculation: declared rates, current weights, candidate buys, rule-based caps. The number you see is reproducible from the inputs you supplied.

---

### 8. SEC Evidence Pipeline

> For eligible US-listed holdings, Compound Income OS supports an explicit, read-only SEC evidence workflow.
>
> The pipeline is gated by reviewed identity inputs and runs only when you start it. It refreshes a snapshot from SEC CompanyFacts, produces an evidence registry and a research backlog, and proposes — but never silently applies — updates to your fundamentals master. You decide what enters the master.
>
> *Read-only. Reviewed. Optional.*

---

### 9. Data Quality Gates

> Every score, ranking, and report is built on top of an explicit data-quality layer.
>
> Coverage statuses — **COVERED**, **PARTIAL**, **REVIEW**, **NO_MATCH**, **MISSING_DATA**, **INSUFFICIENT_INPUTS**, **INSUFFICIENT_HISTORY** — are first-class outputs, not warnings buried in a log. Holdings with insufficient evidence don’t get force-classified into a buy or sell bucket. They get flagged for review and stay visible until you address them.
>
> The default behavior of the system, when in doubt, is to remain conservative and surface the gap.

---

### 10. Decision Journal

> Each monthly run produces a Markdown decision report and a journal entry: the candidates considered, the rules applied, the cash logic, the review cases, the data gaps.
>
> Two years from now, you can open last August’s file and reconstruct exactly why a position was added, deferred, or left alone. The decision journal is not a memory aid. It is the audit trail your future self will need.

## F. Compliance-Safe Claim Review

Wording the brand should never use, with calmer, more accurate alternatives. This table is the single source of truth for copy review on every section, blog post, and outbound message.

| Risky wording | Why it’s risky | Safer alternative |
|---|---|---|
| *Investment recommendations* | Implies regulated advice | Decision-support rankings |
| *Buy signals* | Trading-bot framing; implies certainty | Rule-based candidate status |
| *Sell signals* | Same | Review cases / rule-based exit candidates |
| *Beats the market* | Return promise | Rules-based ranking with visible coverage |
| *Generates alpha* | Performance claim | Documents process and data quality |
| *AI tells you what to buy* | Misrepresents the product; advice framing | Applies your rules to your data, transparently |
| *Robo-advisor / automated advisor* | Regulatory red flag | Local research workflow |
| *Automated rebalancing* | Implies execution | Rebalance proposal artifacts |
| *Real-time portfolio optimization* | Overstated; not the product | Reproducible monthly portfolio analysis |
| *Verified fundamentals* | Implies an authority the system doesn’t have | Evidence-backed or reviewed fundamentals |
| *Guaranteed returns / safe returns* | Return promise | No outcome claim made |
| *Fully automated coverage* | Contradicts the evidence-only principle | Coverage is computed; gaps remain visible |
| *Smart insights* | SaaS filler; vague | Explicit rules, visible inputs, deterministic outputs |
| *Wealth-building platform* | Promise + advice framing | Long-term research and decision-support system |
| *Tax optimization* | Implies tax advice | Cost and tax artifacts where evidenced inputs exist |
| *Connected to your broker* | Execution implication | Reads your broker exports as local files |
| *Beat your benchmark* | Performance promise | Compare against a benchmark you configure |
| *Crypto / BTC integration* | Off-brand and out of scope | (Not used.) |
| *Prediction markets / forecasts* | Off-brand and out of scope | (Not used.) |
| *Investing made easy* | Trivializes the discipline | Investing made reproducible |
| *Effortless / seamless / supercharge* | Generic SaaS hype | Reproducible, deterministic, visible |

### Always include, somewhere visible
- “Local-first.”
- “No broker execution.”
- “Not investment advice.”
- “Open-source core.”

### Never include
- Specific return figures, percentages, or backtested performance claims on the homepage.
- Screenshots of identifiable real portfolio data without sanitization.
- Customer logos or testimonials that imply professional advisory use.
- Anything that frames the system as a substitute for a financial advisor, accountant, or broker.
